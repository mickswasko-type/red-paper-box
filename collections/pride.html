On the Record:
One Paper, the Long March to Marriage

Between 2008 and 2016, RedEye Chicago put LGBT life on its cover again and again — Prop 8 and Boystown, civil unions and DADT, Illinois saying I do, the Supreme Court saying everyone, and the morning after Orlando. Scroll the front pages a free daily ran while a country changed its mind.

A decade. One front page at a time.

This is a single thread pulled from a much larger archive — roughly 800 complete editions of RedEye Chicago, saved before they could disappear. The same approach works for any thread: the Cubs drought, the CTA, a Chicago summer. Pick a topic, the archive remembers.

Covers shown are the ones whose pages led with or flagged LGBT life and the marriage fight. Not every mention — just the ones that made the front.
