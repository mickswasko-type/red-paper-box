Chicago News Archive

About this project

This is a personal, non-commercial project — a one-person effort to preserve and revisit the front pages of a free Chicago daily tabloid that published from 2008 to 2016, saved before they could quietly disappear.

It's offered for free. It carries no advertising and earns no money. It exists for historical, educational, and research purposes — a way to look back at how the city read its mornings, one front page at a time, and to study a decade of local news design and headlines.

On rights & affiliation

This archive is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the original publication, its former publisher, Tribune Publishing, or any successor or related entity. All names, trademarks, and copyrights in the underlying material remain the property of their respective owners.

The front-page images were gathered from the publicly available Internet Archive. Nothing here is intended to compete with, substitute for, or diminish the market for the original work — it is preservation and commentary, offered in good faith.

Concerns or takedown

If you hold rights to this material and would like something corrected or removed, I'll respond promptly and in good faith. This is a personal undertaking, not a business, and I'm happy to work things out — please reach out.
